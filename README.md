### About Me

I'm a results-driven Cloud and DevOps Engineer with a strong foundation in Kubernetes, cloud infrastructure, automation, and security. My expertise lies in building scalable, resilient, and secure cloud-native architectures, optimizing CI/CD pipelines, and implementing observability and security best practices.

I hold the Prometheus Certified Associate (PCA) certification, am a member of MIET and MBCS, and have published IEEE research on Cloud and Kubernetes systems.

**Innovation & Problem-Solving**
My work revolves around cloud-native microservices, secure data transmission, and IoT-based cloud solutions. I focus on designing high-performance distributed systems while ensuring data security, compliance, and operational efficiency.

**Professional Mindset**
I thrive in fast-paced, collaborative environments, continuously learning and applying new technologies to enhance automation, security, and system reliability. My goal is to bridge the gap between development and operations, ensuring seamless scalability, monitoring, and resilience in modern cloud environments.
